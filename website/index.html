Self-hosting

Your data never leaves your perimeter.

Four deployment modes, from offline laptop to classified data centre. You pick the isolation radius, we ship the signed binary, the Helm chart and the Terraform modules. Zero runtime dependency on our infrastructure.

  1. 01 Max isolation

    Air-gap · Offline

    Single binary on a classified workstation or isolated server. Models transferred via signed USB. No network required.

    • SCIF / classified-room compatible
    • SHA-256 verified model weights
    • Zero active network services
    $ diaspor-agent serve --air-gap --models ./models.tar
  2. 02 Your infra

    On-premise

    Your server, your data centre, your Kubernetes cluster. Helm chart ships. Atomic git-tag updates. No outbound bridge required.

    • k8s ≥ 1.28 · OpenShift compatible
    • NVIDIA · AMD · Apple Silicon GPUs
    • Offline update flow available
    $ helm install diaspor diaspor/diaspor --version 0.1
  3. 03 Recommended

    Your Canadian cloud tenancy

    Diaspor deployed inside your AWS · Azure · GCP account, Canadian region. You keep the keys (BYOK), the logs, the bill.

    • Official Terraform modules
    • BYOK · KMS · HSM
    • Co-managed or self-managed
    module "diaspor" { source = "stonyp90/diaspor/aws" }
  4. 04 Diaspor-managed

    Our Canadian cloud

    Multi-tenant deployment in AWS ca-central-1. For firms that don't want to operate. Per-tenant encryption, strict logical isolation, audit-log export.

    • 99.9% SLA · QC business hours

    • Cost-plus operating model

FAQ

Answers procurement asks for

The questions every privacy officer, CIO, and security reviewer sends us first.

Where is our data physically stored?

All Diaspor-managed storage and AI inference runs in AWS ca-central-1 (Montréal). Your files never cross the Canadian border. We do not use US-region providers, even for queue or metadata. Sub-processor list is published quarterly and available under NDA before contract signature.

What's your SOC 2 / ISO 27001 status?

SOC 2 Type II is on the 2026 roadmap. ISO 27001 follows in the next 12-month cycle. Loi 25 alignment is architectural from day one: data residency, audit logging, and personal-data classification are part of the design. Personal-data scanner, consent registry, retention engine, and breach-log generation are rolling out through 2026. PIPEDA alignment carries over from the Loi 25 work.

How is Diaspor licensed?

The model weights (diaspor-speech-v1, diaspor-vision-v1, diaspor-pose-3d-v1, diaspor-prosody-v1, diaspor-credibility-v1, diaspor-judge-v1, diaspor-rag-v1) are proprietary — trained in-house on Canadian legal, security and sport corpora — and ship signed (SHA-256 + Ed25519) into your tenancy under our standard MSA. The runtime binaries are signed builds with full audit logs; full source review is available under NDA for security teams. Internal use is unrestricted; redistribution to third parties requires a commercial agreement.

What happens to our data if we stop paying?

Your storage is your storage — Diaspor never touches the underlying buckets. You disconnect the integration; your data stays exactly where it was in S3, Azure, GCS, or NAS. We retain anonymized usage metadata for 30 days, then permanently delete. Export of embeddings, transcripts, and AI artifacts is available on demand, in standard formats (JSON, Parquet, SRT, VTT, plain text).

Do you support French — really?

French is a first-class language across the product: UI, support, documentation, bilingual MSA, and every AI surface (transcription, translation, OCR, RAG, non-verbal scoring). diaspor-speech-v1 is designed for Québec French — courtroom contexts, trades vocabulary and regional accents — and calibrates with our founding-cohort partners through supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, not a generic English-first model with French bolted on. Bill 96 alignment for Québec public-sector procurement is part of the contractual posture from launch.

What's the support model?

Support tiers active from public launch: Pro — email, 1 business-day response, EN/FR; Team — shared Slack, 4-hour business-hours response, EN/FR; Enterprise — dedicated point of contact, 1-hour P1 response business-hours, optional 24×7, bilingual phone, quarterly product review. In private beta, every design partner has a direct line to the founder.

Can you sign our DPA / contractual agreement?

Yes. We're preparing a bilingual MSA + DPA template aligned with Québec public-sector procurement frameworks (BSP, SQI). Custom redlines are part of the Team / Enterprise engagement. SOC 2 evidence becomes available under NDA once Type II completes in 2026; in the interim we share an internal controls walk-through and our roadmap.

Founder-led and bootstrapped from Montréal. Detailed company information, founder background, and roadmap available under NDA for procurement and partnership conversations. Reach hello@diaspor.io to start a conversation.
